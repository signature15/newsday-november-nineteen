# Design Notes

## Design Notes

The layout follows the grammar of a print front page on a 1024x1024 canvas: a cream newsprint ground sets a warm, analog tone, and the huge masthead spanning the top announces the nameplate and the edition date, Thursday 19 November 2026, as the page's single loudest element. The center of gravity is the world map panel, where numbered pins place each story on its home continent so the reader can navigate geographically before reading a single headline. Around and below that anchor, the thirty-three reconciled stories are ranked by weight - three hero cards first, then progressively smaller cards, then short region-tagged evening briefs - creating a clear top-to-bottom hierarchy that lets a skim take ten seconds and a full read take ten minutes. The date-check footer closes the page by separating real 19 November events from Wednesday carryover, acting as both an editorial footnote and a self-audit of the edition's accuracy.
