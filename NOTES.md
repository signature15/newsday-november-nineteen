# Design Notes - Newsday Thursday 19 November 2026

The page is a cream broadsheet with one job: prove, at a glance, that it is
Thursday 19 November 2026 and that every number on it reconciles. A huge
masthead date carries the day; a world map panel carries numbered pins that
tie each region to the first story number of that region; the day-in-numbers
strip only quotes figures that appear as stories on the same page.

## Why the map pins tie to story numbers

The pins are keyed to story numbers rather than city names so a reader can
jump from the map to the exact card without a legend lookup - the number on
the pin is the number of the first story in that region's group.

## Sources

Reuters, AP, AFP, BBC, Bloomberg, Kyodo, ICJ press releases, USGS.

## Layout phases

Masthead and region tabs, day-in-numbers strip, three hero cards, five
secondary cards, five tertiary cards, then the map panel with the numbered
story briefs and the date-check footer.
